Pro Tips for Outreach

  • Best time to send: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM recipient's time
  • Always personalize: Customize the first line to show you've done your research
  • Keep it brief: Aim for under 150 words to respect their time
  • Speed to lead: Include WhatsApp for faster, more direct communication
  • The Rule of Two: Follow up once, then move on if there's no interest
