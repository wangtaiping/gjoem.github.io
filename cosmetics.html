Makeup OEM FAQ

Can you create custom foundation shades for my brand?

Yes. Our color lab develops custom shade ranges with micronized pigments for smooth application. We support 40+ inclusive shades with spectrophotometer QC (±0.5 ΔE tolerance). Typical shade development takes 5-7 days.

What is the minimum order for makeup products?

Foundation starts at 1,000 units, lip products at 2,000 units, eye makeup at 2,000 units, and powders/blush at 1,000 units. We support indie brands with flexible low MOQ options.

Do you offer clean/vegan makeup formulations?

Yes. We provide vegan, cruelty-free, paraben-free, talc-free, and gluten-free makeup formulations. ECOCERT/COSMOS certifiable ingredients available for natural makeup lines.

What makeup formats can you manufacture?

We manufacture liquid foundations, cushion compacts, lipsticks (bullet/liquid/gloss), mascara, eyeliners, eyeshadow palettes, cream blushes, contour sticks, setting sprays, and pressed/loose powders.

How long does makeup product development take?

Shade matching: 5-7 days. Formula development: 2-3 weeks. Sample production: 3-5 days. Bulk production: 15-25 days after sample approval. Total timeline: approximately 4-6 weeks.
