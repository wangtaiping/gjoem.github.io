Knowledge Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our cosmetics OEM/ODM manufacturing process, capabilities, and terms.

What types of cosmetics can Huabaotang manufacture?
We offer comprehensive manufacturing for skincare (serums, creams, masks, cleansers), body care (scrubs, oils, lotions), hair care (shampoos, conditioners, masks), and fragrance (perfume, diffusers, candles). Our library includes over 10,000 mature formulas.
What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
MOQs depend on the product type: Serums/creams (1,000 units), sheet masks (10,000), hair care (2,000), and fragrance (500). We offer flexible solutions for startup brands.
What certifications does your factory hold?
Our facility is ISO 22716 and GMPC (US/EU) certified. We also hold a China Production License and ensure compliance with US MoCRA, EU CPNP, and GCC standards.
Do you offer custom formulations?
Yes, our R&D team of 50+ scientists provides full custom formulation services, including bespoke bases, active ingredients, textures, and scents. Development typically takes 2-4 weeks.
What is the typical lead time?
Sampling takes 3-7 days. Bulk production generally ranges from 15-30 days after sample approval, depending on complexity and order size.
Can you help with packaging design?
Absolutely. We assist with primary packaging (bottles, jars, tubes), secondary packaging (boxes), labels, and printing through our in-house design team.
Do you provide free samples?
We provide 2-3 formula samples free of charge. Custom development samples may incur a small fee, which is credited back against your bulk order.
What payment terms do you accept?
We accept T/T (30% deposit, 70% before shipment), L/C for large orders, and Trade Assurance on Alibaba.com.
How do you handle international shipping?
We provide full logistics support, including FOB Guangzhou, CIF, and DDP terms. We partner with major freight forwarders for sea, air, and express delivery.
Can I visit your factory?
Yes! We welcome visits to our factory in Huadu, Guangzhou. Virtual tours are also available for international clients.
How do you ensure product quality?
We implement strict QC protocols, including raw material inspection, in-process monitoring, and finished product testing (stability, microbial, heavy metal). All batches are traceable.
Can you formulate vegan/cruelty-free products?
Yes, we can develop 100% plant-based and cruelty-free products. We do not conduct animal testing.
Do you offer private label services?
Yes, we provide full private label services, including formula selection, packaging, branding, and regulatory registration support.
What is your R&D capability?
With over 50 scientists and 10,000+ mature formulas, we develop 20+ new formulas monthly and maintain partnerships with Korean and European ingredient suppliers.
Which countries do you export to?
We export to over 30 countries across North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Latin America, and Africa.
How do I start a project with Huabaotang?
Contact us with your requirements → Our R&D team provides formulation options → We send samples for approval → Production begins → Final delivery.
Do you provide regulatory compliance support?
Yes, we assist with EU CPNP notification, US MoCRA registration, ASEAN notification, and GCC compliance to ensure your brand enters markets legally.
What makes Huabaotang different from other factories?
Our 20+ years of experience, GMPC/ISO certifications, Korean R&D partnerships, and deep market trend insights set us apart as a premium manufacturing partner.
Can you manufacture organic/natural certified products?
Yes, we support ECOCERT/COSMOS, USDA Organic, and China Organic standards by sourcing certified ingredients and following strict manufacturing protocols.
