From Kitchen to Sephora — A Clean Skincare Brand

Background

US-based founder with homemade formulas looking for a professional manufacturing partner to scale for retail distribution.

Challenge

Strict clean beauty certification requirements, a tight 90-day timeline for a 6-SKU launch, and the need for premium, sustainable packaging.

Solution

Developed 6 custom formulas (serum, moisturizer, eye cream, cleanser, toner, mask) using ECOCERT-compatible ingredients. Implemented airless packaging and custom luxury boxes. Provided full stability testing and FDA registration support.

Result

Launched on time. Successfully featured in the Sephora Clean Beauty section. Generated $200K in revenue in the first month.

Middle Eastern Perfume Brand Expansion

Background

A leading Dubai-based perfume house seeking to expand its presence into the European luxury market.

Challenge

Navigating EU CPNP compliance, developing an alcohol-free luxury formula, and creating high-end packaging that appeals to European aesthetics.

Solution

Created custom EDP formulas by Grasse-trained perfumers. All scents are Phthalate-free and IFRA-compliant. Sourced Italian glass bottles with magnetic caps. Handled the entire EU CPNP notification process.

Result

Successfully entered 3 major European markets. Initial order of 15,000 units sold through rapidly, with quarterly repeat orders established.

Brazilian Hair Care Influencer to Brand

Background

A Brazilian Instagram beauty influencer with 800K followers wanting to launch a signature product line.

Challenge

Required low initial MOQs to test the market, extremely quick turnaround to capitalize on social media trends, and highly photogenic packaging.

Solution

Developed a Keratin shampoo, conditioner, and hair mask line with a custom tropical scent (açaí + coconut). Designed "Instagram-ready" gradient bottles. Offered a 1,000 unit MOQ with 4-week delivery.

Result

Sold out the first batch in just 72 hours. Scaled to 10,000 units for the first reorder and expanded to a full 8-SKU line.
