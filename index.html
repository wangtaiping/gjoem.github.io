FAQ

Common Questions for Brand Owners

What products does Huabaotang specialize in?

We specialize in high-end skincare (serums, creams), professional hair care (shampoos, masks), and luxury fragrances (perfume, reed diffusers) for global markets.

What certifications does the factory hold?

Our facility is ISO 22716 and GMPC (US/EU) certified, meeting strict compliance standards for the USA (MoCRA) and EU (CPNP) markets.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ varies by category. Generally, serums start at 1,000 units and sheet masks at 10,000 units. We support emerging "Indie Beauty" brands with flexible low MOQ options.

Do you provide custom formulations?

Yes. We offer access to 10,000+ mature formulas and provide bespoke R&D services to tailor active ingredients and efficacy claims to your brand needs.
