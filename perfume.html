Perfume Concentration Guide

From high-concentration luxury to light mists — each tier serves a different customer segment and occasion.

Parfum Extrait

20-40% · 12h+

Ultra-premium. Pure perfume extract. Niche luxury brands. Smallest spray volume, richest scent.

Eau de Parfum (EDP)

15-20% · 6-8h

Most popular premium tier. Best value for indie brands. Strong projection, great longevity.

Eau de Toilette (EDT)

8-15% · 4-6h

Daily fresh wear. Mass retail friendly. Popular in Europe and Middle East markets.

Eau de Cologne (EDC)

4-8% · 2-3h

Refreshing splash. Citrus-forward. Gender-neutral. Perfect for tropical markets.

Perfume OEM FAQ

Can you create a custom perfume scent for my brand?

Yes. Our Grasse-trained perfumers work with you to create a unique signature scent. We develop 3-5 fragrance candidates based on your brand direction, with detailed top/heart/base note breakdowns. Typical development time is 2 weeks.

What is the MOQ for custom perfume manufacturing?

EDP starts at 500 units, EDT at 1,000 units, and perfume oil roll-ons at 500 units. We offer flexible MOQ for indie brands and can scale as your brand grows.

Are your fragrances IFRA compliant?

Yes. All our fragrances are formulated in strict compliance with IFRA (International Fragrance Association) standards. We also support EU allergen labeling, phthalate-free and vegan formulations.

What perfume formats can you manufacture?

We manufacture EDP sprays, EDT sprays, perfume oil roll-ons, solid perfume compacts, hair & body mists, and discovery gift sets. Bottle sizes from 5ml minis to 100ml full size.

How much does custom perfume manufacturing cost?

Formula development is typically $500-2,000. Unit costs range from $1.50-4.00 depending on concentration, packaging, and order volume. A 500-unit EDP project typically costs $2,000-4,000 total including formula, production, and basic packaging.
