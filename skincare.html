Skincare OEM FAQ

What skincare products can you manufacture?

We manufacture serums, face creams, sheet masks, cleansers, toners, eye creams, face oils, and lip care products. Each category has multiple formats — water-based serums, oil-based serums, gel creams, balm textures, bio-cellulose masks, hydrogel masks, foam cleansers, micellar waters, and more.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for skincare?

Face serums start at 1,000 units, moisturizers at 2,000 units, and sheet masks at 10,000 units. We offer flexible MOQ options for indie brands and startups — contact us to discuss your specific needs.

Do you create custom skincare formulations?

Yes. Our 50+ R&D scientists develop custom formulations based on your brand requirements — target active ingredients, texture preferences, scent, and efficacy claims. Typical development cycle is 2-4 weeks with 2-3 sample iterations.

Can you formulate clean/vegan/cruelty-free skincare?

Absolutely. We offer vegan, cruelty-free, paraben-free, sulfate-free, and silicone-free formulations. We can also support ECOCERT/COSMOS certification requirements for organic and natural product lines.

How long does it take from formula to delivery?

Typical timeline: 2-4 weeks for formula development, 3-7 days for sampling, 15-30 days for bulk production after sample approval, and 7-30 days for shipping depending on destination and method. Total: approximately 6-10 weeks.
