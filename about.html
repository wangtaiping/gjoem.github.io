About Huabaotang

Our Legacy of Excellence

Your Trusted Cosmetics Manufacturing Partner Since 2002

Our Journey

Beauty Rooted in Science

Founded in 2002, Guangzhou Huabaotang Biotechnology has evolved from a local laboratory into a global leader in cosmetics OEM/ODM manufacturing. For over two decades, we have remained committed to the intersection of traditional wisdom and modern biotechnology, creating products that define market trends and empower brand success.

Our journey is driven by a passion for beauty and a relentless pursuit of quality. Today, we serve over 30 countries worldwide, providing a one-stop solution from innovative R&D to large-scale production, ensuring every product meets the highest international standards of safety and efficacy.
